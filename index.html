The Difference

The large exporters ship their own labels. We ship everyone else's.

Groups like Treasury Wine Estates export at scale because they own the labels — but that scale only reaches their own wineries. Independent family vineyards are locked out, because a container built around ten cases doesn't work economically on its own.

The Old Model

One producer, one shipment

Export only works at volumes a single boutique vineyard can rarely produce — so independent, family-run wineries are priced out of international markets entirely, no matter how good the wine.

Sapphire Panda

Twenty producers, one pallet

We combine small lots — often just ten or so cases — from dozens of independent vineyards onto a single consolidated shipment. The economics work because no one producer has to carry it alone.
