The Difference

Same producers, same shipment, every time. Our engine ends the repetition.

Traditional export runs on one broker relationship and a static shortlist. Our engine runs on AI agents that re-score millions of combinations across the whole independent market — for every buyer, and every vineyard, on every order.

For buyers
The Old Model

Same producers, container after container

One broker relationship, one static shortlist. Buyers get whatever's already on the list — the same handful of labels, shipment after shipment.

Sapphire Panda

A different shipment every time

AI agents re-score your brief against the whole independent market on every order — millions of combinations — so what's matched changes as stock, vintages and vineyards do.

For vineyards
The Old Model

No full pallet, no export

Export normally needs a full pallet of your own wine. Small, family-run vineyards without enough stock to fill one alone are locked out entirely.

Sapphire Panda

Small lots, consolidated by AI

Our agents combine matched lots from multiple vineyards onto a single pallet, so producers who could never hit volume alone can still export.
