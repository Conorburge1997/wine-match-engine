How It Works

From a buyer's brief to a matched, verified shipment.

A network of specialised AI agents handles the matching, the verification and the logistics, coordinated automatically, end to end.

01

You list your requirements.

Style, region, price band and volume, no wine jargon required. Your exact brief becomes the seed for a build made only for you.

02

Our AI matches it.

Machine learning agents score your brief against our vineyard partners' data across millions of combinations, curating a shortlist built around exactly what you asked for, for you to choose from.

03

Order is generated.

Once you pick from your shortlist, automated agents confirm stock, generate SKUs and clear destination-country paperwork. A live purchase order built with no manual handling.

04

Vineyards are requested.

Independent vineyards across regions confirm and fulfil the order in parallel, coordinated by AI agents running thousands of checks behind the scenes.

05

Pallet is consolidated.

A logistics agent builds a single load from multiple wines and multiple vineyards, a highly complex, one-of-a-kind build, packed and scheduled through to port automatically.

06

Agents clear customs.

Our AI-driven agents handle every form, permit and compliance check across borders. Real complexity, fully streamlined, so you never touch a single document.

07

You receive it.

One consolidated pallet splits out automatically to every buyer on arrival, with full complexity resolved before it ever reaches you.

About

Why Sapphire Panda exists.

Sapphire Panda is built to close that gap. An AI matching engine pairs each buyer's requirements with the best-fit vineyards across Australia, whatever their size, and consolidates them into one pallet and one order, with the complexity handled.

Conor grew up less than an hour from the award-winning Yarra Valley, where he frequented independent vineyards rich in history and wine variety, with multiple awards to their name. Whilst working overseas, he saw a market dominated by large-scale commercial wine from a few dominant brands, with small and medium-sized Australian vineyards barely represented.

After attending the Belt and Road Summit in Hong Kong and events with Business Network International, he spoke with buyers at clubs, venues and restaurants across Europe and Asia, who wanted boutique, distinctive Australian wine but had no reliable way to find it.

Back in Australia, vineyards described the other side of the gap. Minimum order quantities, licensing and paperwork, and limited advertising spend compared with commercial leaders stood between them and export, and most had never shipped a bottle overseas. Award-winning wine was effectively gatekept from the buyers who wanted it.

Conor
FOUNDER

Conor has worked in management consulting and the consumer goods industry, primarily across go-to-market strategy and AI transformation, in Australia, the UK, the Czech Republic and Poland.

Most recently at Deloitte, he worked on the implementation of large-scale AI across supply chain and operations for public and private sector clients.

Prior to this, Conor studied a Bachelor and Master of Business at Monash University in Melbourne, Australia.

Sapphire Panda applies that experience to wine: AI-led matching, so the best wine reaches buyers on merit rather than marketing budget.
